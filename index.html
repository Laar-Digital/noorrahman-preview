Questions

Ask your own
Which areas does Noor cover?

Centennial, Greenwood Village, Englewood, Westminster, Highlands Ranch, Denver and nearby Denver metro.

Do I need a pre-approval before touring?

Sellers usually expect one with an offer, so talk to a lender early. Noor can tell you when.

How is a listing price set?

From recent sales of similar homes nearby. You see those sales before you decide.

Who else is involved in a purchase?

Usually a lender, an inspector, an appraiser and a title company. Noor keeps their deadlines in step.
