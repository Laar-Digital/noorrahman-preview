History and homes

How each placetook shape.

  • Centennial

    Incorporated 2001

    Home to Centennial Center Park.

  • Greenwood Village

    Incorporated 1950

    Next to the Denver Tech Center, and home to Fiddler’s Green Amphitheatre.

  • Englewood

    Incorporated 1903

    Directly south of Denver. Bungalows, ranch homes and the mid-century modern Arapahoe Acres, built 1949 to 1957.

  • Westminster

    A new centre

    The former mall site is being rebuilt as Downtown Westminster.

  • Highlands Ranch

    First homes 1981

    An unincorporated, master-planned community, with Highlands Ranch Mansion and Historic Park.

  • Denver

    Older central neighbourhoods

    Victorian, Denver Square and bungalow homes.
