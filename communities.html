How each place took shape

Centennial
Incorporated as a city in 2001.
Greenwood Village
Incorporated in 1950. Home to Fiddler’s Green Amphitheatre.
Englewood
Incorporated in 1903, directly south of Denver. Bungalows, ranch homes and mid-century modern Arapahoe Acres, built 1949 to 1957.
Westminster
The former mall site is being rebuilt as Downtown Westminster.
Highlands Ranch
Unincorporated and master-planned, with first homes in 1981.
Denver
Victorian, Denver Square and bungalow homes in older central neighbourhoods.
