Questions

Good toknow first.

Ask your own
Do I need a pre-approval before touring?

Sellers usually expect one with an offer. Talk to a lender early.

What is earnest money?

A good-faith deposit made once your offer is accepted. It is normally applied to your costs at closing.

Can I ask for repairs after the inspection?

Yes, by the inspection deadline in your contract. Noor can walk you through it.

What are closing costs?

Loan, title and recording fees paid at closing. Your lender lists them in a Loan Estimate.
