Buyer questions

Before youmake an offer.

Ask your own question
Do I need a pre-approval before touring?

Not to tour. Sellers usually expect one with an offer, so talk to a lender early.

What is earnest money?

A deposit, usually held by the title company, due by the date in your contract. It is credited to you at closing.

Can I ask for repairs after the inspection?

You can ask by the inspection objection deadline in your contract, request a credit instead, or end the contract. Noor can walk you through it.

What are closing costs?

Loan, title and recording fees paid at closing. Your lender lists them in a Loan Estimate.
