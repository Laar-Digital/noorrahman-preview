Ask your own question
What does cash flow mean here?

Rent left after the mortgage, tax, insurance, HOA dues, vacancy and upkeep. The estimator above shows it.

Can I rent out a condo or townhome?

It depends on the HOA. Its documents may limit rentals, so read them first.

Are short-term rentals allowed?

Rules and licensing differ by city and HOA. Noor can walk you through it.

Can I buy a home with a tenant in it?

Yes. Read the current lease and deposit terms before you offer.
