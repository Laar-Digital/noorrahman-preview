Seller questions

Before thesign goes up.

Ask your own question
How is a listing price set?

From recent sales of similar homes nearby. You see those sales before you decide.

Do I have to fix what the inspection finds?

No. You can agree to repairs, offer a credit or say no.

Do I tell buyers about known problems?

Yes. Colorado sellers must disclose known material defects, usually on the Seller’s Property Disclosure form. Noor can walk you through it.

When do I receive the sale money?

At closing, through the title company, after your loan payoff and closing costs.
